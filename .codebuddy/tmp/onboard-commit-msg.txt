build(examples): hide maintainer options and add a minimal onboarding example

A product review of the consumer surface found two places where the surface was larger than the
substance. Both are fixed here without changing a single default.

- Ten configure switches were presented to every consumer: BUILD_TESTS/EXAMPLES, BUILD_CORE,
  BUILD_REMOTE_ACCESS, BUILD_WIDGETS_ADAPTER, BUILD_QUICK_ADAPTER, WITH_VNC, BUILD_SPIKES,
  WITH_GBM and WITH_RKMPP. They are maintainer switches, not consumer choices - the
  consumer-simplicity contract is that a consumer names HyRemote::RemoteAccess and selects nothing
  else - so they are mark_as_advanced() now. Measured: `cmake -L` lists six HYREMOTE_ options
  instead of sixteen, and every hidden one stays reachable with `cmake -LA` or by passing it
  explicitly. The call sits at the end of the file deliberately: mark_as_advanced() has no effect on
  an option that has not been declared yet, which the first attempt got wrong and the measurement
  caught.

- The examples are release evidence and read like it: 131-274 lines each, mostly a READY /
  CLIENT_COUNT / POLICY_* protocol and an input probe, with five to fifteen lines of actual
  HyRemote use. Someone learning the product had to read the instrumentation to find the four lines
  that matter. examples/hello-remote is about thirty lines of the normal consumer path, explicitly
  outside the E1-E6 evidence matrix, and is linked from the C++ getting-started guide so it can
  actually be found.

The example prints with std::endl rather than '\n' on purpose: its output is redirected to a file by
a scripted smoke check, and an unflushed line is lost when that check stops the process.

Verified: the example builds and runs - it listens on 127.0.0.1:5921 and says so - the full suite
passes, and the ten release gates pass with the gate-pinned option lines untouched.
