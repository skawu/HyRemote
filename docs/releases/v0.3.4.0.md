  - Qt 6.8.3：C++/Widgets、C++/Quick、QML、Generic 为 Supported；QPA 仅精确 6.8.3 私有 ABI（Limited）。
  - Qt 5.15.2：C++/Widgets 为 Limited（Runtime、Tool、示例、安装消费者与 qmake 消费者）；Quick 为

- 显式 Kit 选择：版本/编译器/架构一次选定，混合 major 与缓存混用 fail-closed；Qt 6.5/6.12 检出即

- RFB 协议正确性：#158 的精确 3.8（3.7 在 security 列表前拒绝）与有界输入缓冲、Normal 阶段
  5s 部分消息进度截止 / 10s post-init 有效性截止；静态未完成增量请求不受影响。

- 默认监听 `0.0.0.0:5921`，远程输入默认关闭；默认认证关闭，RFB 传输不加密。

# English

- Version: `V0.3.4.0` (candidate, not accepted)
- Release branch: `release/v0.3.4.0` (created after the candidate is frozen)
- Current development target: `develop` (the exact candidate SHA is fixed by #95 at freeze)
- Reference platform: **Windows x86_64**
- Target Qt families: **Qt 5.15.2 (msvc2019_64) and Qt 6.8.3 (msvc2022_64)**; Qt 6.5/6.12 remain post-V1-GA (#605)

## What this candidate contains

- Windows Qt 5.15.2 and Qt 6.8.3 exact-Kit cells on one Core and one Shared Runtime:
  - Qt 6.8.3: C++/Widgets, C++/Quick, QML and Generic are Supported; QPA is Limited to the exact
    6.8.3 private ABI.
  - Qt 5.15.2: C++/Widgets is Limited (Runtime, Tool, examples, installed and qmake consumers); Quick
    is Experimental (internal opt-in only, normal entry hidden); QML and Generic are Unsupported;
    QPA is Not applicable.
- Explicit Kit selection: version/compiler/architecture are chosen once, mixed majors and cache reuse
  fail closed, and Qt 6.5/6.12 are detected but not qualified.
- Source `add_subdirectory()` and installed `find_package(HyRemote)` / `hyremote_deploy` consumers,
  including the Qt 5 qmake path and wrong-major negatives.
- RFB protocol truth from #158: exact 3.8 (3.7 rejected before any security list), bounded input
  buffering, a 5 s partial-message progress deadline and a 10 s post-init usefulness deadline in the
  Normal phase; a static outstanding incremental request is never treated as idle.
- The bilingual exact-cell matrix is enforced by
  `hyremote-release-readiness-compatibility-cells`; a Qt 5 route cannot be silently raised to
  Supported.

## Security boundary

- Default listener `0.0.0.0:5921`, remote input disabled by default, authentication disabled by
  default and RFB traffic unencrypted.
- Optional legacy VncAuth authenticates the viewer only and adds no encryption; without additional
  encryption and access control this is a trusted-LAN capability.
- TLS/VeNCrypt, cloud relay and a full management plane are out of scope; Qt 5 QML/Generic/QPA are
  outside the support statement.

## Known limitations

- Qt 5.15.2 holds only in its exact bounded cell; other Qt 5 patches and other Qt 5 frontends are not
  implied.
- Qt 5 Quick remains Experimental: capture/input tests still fail and the normal entry is hidden.
- RealVNC Viewer 7.15.1 is the required maintained viewer for this release; other viewers do not
  inherit the result.
- Linux desktop, ARM64/EGLFS and Wayland are user-validation targets, not release gates and not
  Supported.
- The candidate is not frozen: no tag, L2, installer or checksum is claimed here.
